How to use it
  1. Open an example and read the problem. You can press ▶ Play first to see what happens.
  2. Solve it on paper. “Step” boxes are optional checkpoints; “Answer” boxes are what the question asks for.
  3. Press Check & play. Answers within 2% get a ✓, and the animation replays with your answers as dashed shapes.
  4. Stuck? Press Show solution for the full worked steps.
